About us

More than transportation, we deliver trust.

Ganeshay Transport is a road freight business built on one idea: when a customer hands over a load, it becomes our responsibility until it is delivered. Under the leadership of owner Omprakash (Chhotu) Harke, we focus on dependable schedules, careful cargo handling and honest communication.

With 15 years of experience and a fleet of 14 vehicles, we are ready for your next load. From heavy industrial material to commercial cargo, we move freight with powerful multi-axle vehicles and a customer-first mindset.

  • Dedication to every consignment
  • Reliability on every route
  • Customer satisfaction first
  • Responsible, safe transportation
